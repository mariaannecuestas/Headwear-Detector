Why Accessible Headwear Vision Matters to Our Community

Headwear sits at the intersection of daily utility, personal identity, and seasonal protection—from sun-shielding structured caps to cold-weather knit beanies. By turning a custom-trained Teachable Machine model into an instant, zero-cost browser atelier, this platform empowers thrift volunteers, independent designers, and everyday wearers alike.

SECTION 03 // STUDIO CAPTURE NOTES

Tips for Crisp Silhouette Recognition

  • 01. Show the Profile or Crown: Angle caps slightly to reveal both the structured crown and visor brim, or lay knit beanies flat to highlight the folded rib cuff.
  • 02. Balanced Daylight: Soft, even lighting helps the lens distinguish dark navy, black, or charcoal knit textures cleanly.
  • 03. Read the Probability Index: Matches at 80% or higher indicate a clear Runway Match; lower scores prompt adjusting angle or distance.
